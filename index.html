What is bbaccounts?

bbaccounts is a service for creating and managing a user account that can be used to sign in to connected projects and applications. When an app asks you to sign in with bbaccounts, you review the requested permissions (such as your profile or email address), approve or deny access, and can revoke that access later from your account settings.

The purpose of bbaccounts is account management and secure sign-in: registering an account, signing in, managing your profile, connecting sign-in providers, and controlling which applications are authorized to access your information.

How it works

01 / account

Create your account

Register once on bbaccounts to use a single account across connected projects.

02 / consent

Approve access

When a connected app requests sign-in, bbaccounts shows what data is requested and you choose whether to allow it.

03 / manage

Stay in control

You can review and revoke authorized apps, manage your profile, and protect your account from your settings.
