Privacy policy for bbaccounts

Last updated November 26, 2026. This policy explains how bbaccounts collects, uses, and shares information when you use this service.

1. About bbaccounts

bbaccounts is an identity provider and sign-in service. It lets you create an account, sign in, manage your profile and security settings, and authorize connected applications to access information from your account using OAuth 2.0. This instance is operated by the service administrator responsible for this deployment.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide and information generated when you use the service, including:

  • Account information: email address, username, display name, optional description/profile details, avatar or profile images, and account status/role information.
  • Authentication and security data: a password hash (not your plain-text password), recovery code hashes, active sessions, and, if enabled, Guard/TOTP-related security data needed for two-step verification.
  • Connected sign-in providers: if you link a social provider (such as Google, GitHub, Discord, or Yandex), we store the provider name, the provider account identifier, and profile information returned by that provider (such as a username or display information) as needed to connect and display the link according to your settings.
  • Authorization and app access data: when you sign in to a connected application, we record the application, the permissions (scopes) you approved, and issue access/refresh tokens or related metadata (stored securely, such as hashed identifiers where applicable) so the app can access only what you allowed.
  • Profile and community content: information you choose to add or create, such as badges, teams, showcases, projects, friends/connections, comments, likes, tickets/messages, and points or activity records where those features are used.
  • Uploads: files or images you upload (for example avatars or showcase media).
  • Technical and security logs: IP address, browser/user-agent information, timestamps, and audit events (such as sign-ins, security changes, and administrative actions) to operate and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage your account and let you sign in;
  • authenticate you and protect accounts from abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access;
  • display your profile and content according to your visibility and privacy settings;
  • process your authorization requests and share approved data with connected applications;
  • operate service features (such as profiles, teams, notifications, developer projects, and support/moderation tools) and communicate service-related notices;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

4. How we share information

With connected applications you authorize: applications receive only the data you approve on the consent screen, and only for the requested scopes. For example, the profile scope may share profile information (such as display name, username, avatar, or description), and the email scope may share your email address. Other scopes may share additional data only if requested and approved. You can revoke an application's access at any time from your account's authorized apps/settings.

Service operation: the operator of this bbaccounts instance and infrastructure used to host it may process data as needed to run, maintain, and secure the service.

Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

bbaccounts uses first-party cookies that are necessary to operate the service, such as keeping you signed in, protecting forms/sessions, remembering your theme preference, and (if you use multiple accounts on the same device) helping you switch between accounts you have signed into. These cookies are not used for advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Security and audit logs are retained as needed to investigate abuse and protect the service.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data from your account security/settings. Permanently banned accounts may be deleted automatically after 90 days. Some records may be retained longer where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Your choices and controls

Depending on the features available in this instance, you can review and update your profile information, connect or disconnect social sign-in providers, manage visibility/privacy options, review and revoke authorized applications, sign out sessions, and request account deletion from your account settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including hashing passwords, restricting access, and recording security events. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was revised. Continued use of the service after changes means the updated policy applies.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, contact the administrator of this bbaccounts instance.
