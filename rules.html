Rules

Short version: be a real person, do not harm other people, and do not abuse the service.

Accounts

These rules are enforced by the code, not only by an administrator.

Automated
  • One account per person. Email addresses and linked provider identities are unique.
  • No ban evasion. A blocked account cannot sign in or authorize projects until the block ends. Permanent bans also delete the account and the data it owns after the configured purge window.
  • No self-likes or like farming. One like per account per profile. Each like is 2 points; profile owners spend points on showcase slots.
  • No credential sharing. Recovery codes are single-use and sessions can be revoked per device.
  • Respect rate limits. Sign-in, registration, recovery, and token endpoints limit repeated attempts automatically.

Content and projects

These are reviewed by a person when someone reports them.

Reviewed
  • No impersonation. Do not use another person's name, avatar, username, or project identity to mislead people.
  • No harassment, hate, or threats. This includes profile descriptions, showcases, links, and project pages.
  • No illegal or sexual content involving minors. Reports are handled immediately and may be passed to the authorities where required.
  • No malware, phishing, or deceptive OAuth clients. Project domains and redirect URIs must belong to you, and the consent screen must describe what the project really does.
  • No scraping or automated account creation. Do not use the service to harvest profiles, emails, or tokens.
  • Only upload images you have the right to use. Avatars and project icons are validated as real images, but copyright and consent are still your responsibility.
  • Do not publish other people's private data. Contact details belong on your own profile only. The Contact email showcase is opt-in.

When no administrator is online

Automated protections stay on.

Sign-in blocks, rate limits, unique identities, self-like prevention, upload validation, and token scopes work without an administrator. Content reports wait for a person. If something is dangerous, contact the address on the privacy page and include links or screenshots.

Breaking these rules can end in a temporary block, a permanent ban, or deletion of the account and its projects.
